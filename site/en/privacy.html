Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28 · 日本語

vw does not collect any information about its users. It has no usage reporting (telemetry), no ads and no accounts.

What stays on your PC

Settings, logs, labels, search catalogs and registered faces are stored only on your PC (in the settings folder or in the folders that hold your images). They are never sent to the authors or to third parties.

When vw uses the network

vw connects to the network only when you use one of these features.

  • Map: showing the map downloads tile images from the tile server in the settings (OpenStreetMap by default). The server receives your IP address and a User-Agent that names vw and its version. Downloaded tiles are kept on your PC and reused for a while.
  • AI tags, face detection and place search: when you ask vw to download them, it downloads the models, their runtime library and the place-name table from GitHub and Hugging Face. Those servers receive your IP address. Your images are never uploaded; all analysis runs on your PC.
  • Network send: vw sends the images you choose to the destination you configured. You choose the destination; nothing goes to the authors.
  • Links: links such as those in Help open in your default web browser.

Each server handles the information it receives under its own policy.
