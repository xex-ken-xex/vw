title: "User guide"
---

How to use vw, organized by what you want to do. Screen names are those of the English display.

- **[Get started](start.html)**: from installing to viewing photos, adding tags and making a catalog.
- **[View images](view.html)**: lay out the images of a folder and compare them quickly.
- **[Sort photos](sort.html)**: flag and tag with one key, keep the good photos and clear away the rest.
- **[Photos shot in RAW](raw.html)**: see them at once, compare with the camera JPEG and check the exposure.
- **[Find and keep images](find.html)**: register folders as datasets and find them by content or location.
- **[Astrophotography (FITS)](astro.html)**: make faint images easy to see, then calibrate, select and finish them.
- **[Weather and ocean data (NetCDF, HDF5)](earth.html)**: see and measure grids of weather, climate and ocean data with their values and units.
- **[Measure and check](check.html)**: pixel values, clipping, focus and color, the pixel pipeline and batch export.
- **[Settings and keys](reference.html)**: settings, keyboard shortcuts, supported formats and the files vw makes.
- **[FAQ and troubleshooting](faq.html)**

Development and build steps are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/xex-ken-xex/vw/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
